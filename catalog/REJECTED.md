# Rejected guides

Sources looked at and left out of the guide catalogue, each with the reason.

## First batch: researchers' rejects (civilian agencies)

Host notes from the research: fema.gov, ready.gov/cert, cdc.gov, stacks.cdc.gov, weather.gov, noaa.gov, fsis.usda.gov, usda.gov, hhs.gov, osha.gov, aphis.usda.gov, foodsafety.gov and nhtsa.gov answer the app's Dalvik User-Agent with HTTP 403; nrcs.usda.gov and usbr.gov reset the connection. None of their PDFs can be offered from those hosts. No FEMA, CDC or NWS document was checked through the Wayback Machine, so those agencies are not represented yet.

- Trail Construction and Maintenance Notebook (USDA Forest Service MTDC with FHWA, https://www.fs.usda.gov/t-d/pubs/pdfpubs/pdf07232806/pdf07232806dpi72.pdf): inside cover carries FHWA boilerplate "The contents of this report reflect the views of the contractor"; possible contractor authorship, and outside reviewers (IMBA) are credited.
- Complete Guide to Home Canning (USDA NIFA, https://nchfp.uga.edu/papers/guide/INTRO_HomeCanrev0715.pdf): acknowledgments credit Penn State and University of Georgia authors; no public-domain statement; hosted on a university site.
- Aviation Weather Handbook FAA-H-8083-28B (FAA, https://www.faa.gov/sites/faa.gov/files/FAA-H-8083-28B.pdf): prepared under an FAA contract with AvMet Applications, Inc.; contains COMET/UCAR material and outside photo credits.
- Ground Water and the Rural Homeowner (USGS, https://pubs.usgs.gov/gip/7000053/report.pdf): photographs courtesy of Cornell University (third-party content, no public-domain statement for it).
- Drinking Water From Household Wells (EPA, https://19january2017snapshot.epa.gov/sites/production/files/2015-05/documents/epa816k02003.pdf): no public-domain statement in the document, and EPA's disclaimer page only says documents may be freely used for non-commercial, scientific and educational purposes, which is not a public-domain statement. The same reason applies to every EPA document without its own statement.
- SepticSmart Homeowners Guide print file (EPA, https://www.epa.gov/sites/default/files/2015-06/documents/epa_septicsmart_print_guide_homeownersguide508.pdf): EPA policy as above; booklet pages are images, text not readable.
- CERT Program Manager Participant Manual (FEMA, https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-HS5_100-PURL-gpo185416/pdf/GOVPUB-HS5_100-PURL-gpo185416.pdf): program administration, not a practical how-to.
- One Moving Part: The Forest Service Ax Manual (USDA Forest Service, https://fs.usda.gov/t-d/pubs/pdfpubs/pdf18232812P/1823-2812P_AxManual_508_10-21-20c_150dpi.pdf): file is about 70 MB, over the 60 MB limit; the 1999 "An Ax to Grind" PDF is no longer offered.
